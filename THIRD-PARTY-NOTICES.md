photographs are not. Every article picture is a real, freely licensed photograph of a real place,
cropped to the plate size and otherwise unaltered. Each is credited on the plate itself in
`heroCredit`, and in full below.

Public-domain and CC0 pictures carry no conditions. The CC BY pictures require attribution, which
the plate credit and this table together provide; if you remove a picture's credit from the page,
